Cameroon Oncology Center School of Health Sciences (COC-SHS)

Education. Practice. Impact.

The Cameroon Oncology Center School of Health Sciences is part of the African University Institute of Sciences and Technology (AUIST). The School operates from the COC main campus in Douala, providing hands-on training in a real clinical environment.

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate training programs
  • Clinical rotations and practical experience
  • Research and academic mentorship
  • Training the next generation of African health professionals
  • Contributing to a stronger health workforce for Cameroon and the region
