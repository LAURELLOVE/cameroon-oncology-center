Directions to Cameroon Oncology Center

1

From Douala, take the N3 road towards Dibombari.

2

Continue on the N3 passing the Yapak-Bakoko interchange.

3

After Carrefour Palmeraie, turn right toward the COC access road (as shown on the map).

4

Follow the road a short distance. Cameroon Oncology Center will be on your right.
