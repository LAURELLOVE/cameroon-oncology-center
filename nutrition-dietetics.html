Meet Our Nutritionist
GM
Dr. Guimeya MichelleNutritionist

Dr. Guimeya Michelle leads our Nutrition Unit, providing evidence-based nutritional care to patients with cancer. She is dedicated to helping patients and families maintain strength, manage treatment side effects, and improve their overall well-being through personalized nutrition support.

When Should You See Our Nutritionist?
  • Unintentional weight loss
  • Poor appetite or reduced food intake
  • Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea or constipation
  • Difficulty swallowing or eating
  • Significant weight changes during treatment
  • Malnutrition or low body weight
  • Need for guidance on what to eat during cancer treatment
  • Before or after surgery
  • For support during survivorship or palliative care
Our Nutrition Services
  • Nutritional assessment using PG-SGA
  • Individualized dietary counseling and meal planning
  • Nutrition support during chemotherapy, radiotherapy and surgery
  • Management of nutrition-related symptoms (e.g. nausea, vomiting, taste changes)
  • Oral nutritional supplements
  • Enteral nutrition (tube feeding) when needed
  • Parenteral nutrition when indicated
  • Nutrition education for patients and caregivers
  • Long-term follow-up and survivorship nutrition
  • Collaboration with the multidisciplinary team
