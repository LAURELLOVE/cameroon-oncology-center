Common Symptoms We Evaluate
  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Palpitations (irregular heartbeat)
  • Swelling of legs (edema)
  • High blood pressure
  • Fatigue
  • Dizziness or fainting
We Care For
  • Patients starting chemotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy
  • Patients receiving radiotherapy (especially chest/breast irradiation)
  • Pre-operative cardiac evaluation
  • Follow-up during and after cancer treatment
  • General adult cardiovascular diseases
  • Survivorship and long-term heart health
Our Cardiology Services
Cardiology Consultation

Comprehensive evaluation and personalized care

Electrocardiogram (ECG)

Fast and accurate diagnosis of heart rhythm disorders

Echocardiography (2D, Doppler, Strain)

Advanced cardiac imaging to assess heart structure and function

Stress Testing

Evaluation of cardiac performance during physical activity

24/48-Hour Holter Monitoring

Continuous heart rhythm monitoring

Management of Hypertension, Heart Failure, Coronary Artery Disease and Valvular Diseases

Medical treatment and regular follow-up

Cardiovascular Prevention

Lifestyle counseling, risk assessment and early intervention

Nuclear Cardiology (MUGA Scan)

Assessment of heart function when indicated, especially before and during certain cancer treatments

Our Cardio-Oncology Program
  • Baseline cardiac assessment before cancer treatment
  • Risk stratification for cardiotoxicity
  • Monitoring during chemotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy
  • Cardiac evaluation before surgery
  • Early detection and management of treatment-related heart problems
  • Coordination with Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology and other specialists
  • Long-term follow-up for cancer survivors
Our Technology
  • Modern ECG machines
  • Echocardiography with Doppler and strain imaging
  • Stress testing
  • 24/48-hour Holter monitoring
  • Nuclear cardiology (MUGA scan)
  • Tele-cardiology and digital consultation

A Multidisciplinary Approach

We work closely with Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Surgery, Anesthesiology, Nuclear Medicine and Primary Care to provide integrated and personalized care.
