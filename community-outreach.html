About the Cameroon Cancer Foundation (CCF)

The Cameroon Cancer Foundation (CCF) is a non-governmental, non-profit organization dedicated to reducing the cancer burden in Cameroon through screening, awareness, prevention, advocacy and patient support. CCF works closely with the Cameroon Oncology Center (COC), which provides the physicians, specialists and nurses for screening activities.

Learn more at: www.camcancerfoundation.org
