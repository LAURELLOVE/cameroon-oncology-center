Our Services

Gynecology & Gynecologic Oncology

Comprehensive Care for Women. From Prevention to Treatment and Survivorship.

At Cameroon Oncology Center, we provide specialized gynecologic and gynecologic oncology care for women of all ages. Our multidisciplinary team is committed to early detection, advanced treatment and compassionate care for gynecologic cancers, breast cancers and other women's health conditions.

"Compassionate, expert care for women at every stage of life."

Our Gynecology & Gynecologic Oncology Services

Cervical Cancer
  • Screening and early detection
  • Diagnosis and staging
  • Surgery, chemotherapy and radiotherapy
  • Management of precancerous lesions (CIN)
  • Follow-up and survivorship
Ovarian Cancer
  • Evaluation of abdominal or pelvic masses
  • Diagnosis and staging
  • Surgery (debulking)
  • Chemotherapy and targeted therapy
  • Long-term follow-up
Endometrial (Uterine) Cancer
  • Evaluation of abnormal uterine bleeding
  • Diagnosis and staging
  • Surgical management
  • Adjuvant therapy (chemotherapy/radiotherapy)
  • Survivorship care
Vulvar, Vaginal & Rare Gynecologic Cancers
  • Accurate diagnosis and staging
  • Surgery and reconstructive options
  • Chemoradiation when needed
  • Multidisciplinary tumor board review
  • Follow-up and survivorship
Breast Surgery (Benign & Malignant)
  • Mastectomy (total or modified radical)
  • Lumpectomy (breast-conserving surgery)
  • Sentinel lymph node dissection
  • Axillary surgery when indicated
  • Oncoplastic and reconstructive options
  • Multidisciplinary breast cancer care
  • Integration with medical oncology, radiotherapy and survivorship services
General Gynecology
  • Evaluation of abnormal Pap smear
  • Management of pelvic masses and fibroids
  • Menstrual and menopausal disorders
  • Family planning and reproductive health
  • Referral for specialized care when needed

Cervical Cancer Care Pathway

From Screening to Survivorship

Screening & Early DetectionPap smear, HPV testing and community screening (through Cameroon Cancer Foundation)
Diagnosis & BiopsyColposcopy, biopsy and histopathology
StagingPelvic examination, imaging (CT/MRI) and multidisciplinary evaluation
Multidisciplinary ReviewDiscussion at tumor board to develop an individualized treatment plan
TreatmentSurgery and/or chemoradiation based on stage and patient factors
Follow-up & SurvivorshipRegular monitoring, management of side effects and long-term support

In Partnership with Cameroon Cancer Foundation

Through community outreach and screening programs, the Cameroon Cancer Foundation (CCF) provides free cervical cancer screening and education across Cameroon. COC works closely with CCF to ensure that women with abnormal results receive timely diagnostic evaluation and treatment.
