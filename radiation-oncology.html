2
Simulation

A CT scan (Philips Big Bore) is performed in the treatment position.

3
Planning

Your treatment is designed using advanced software and reviewed by our clinical and physics team.

4
Treatment

Each session is painless and usually takes only a few minutes.

5
Follow-up

We monitor your progress and manage any side effects.

Quality & Safety

Precision requires more than technology. It requires a culture of quality. Every radiation treatment at COC involves multiple layers of review, including radiation oncologist review, medical physics treatment-plan verification, machine quality assurance, patient-specific QA where applicable, image guidance and multidisciplinary discussion. Treatment plans are developed with attention to internationally recognized normal-tissue dose constraints, including QUANTEC guidance where applicable, to optimize tumor coverage while limiting radiation exposure to organs at risk.
