Our Four Levels of Inpatient Care

Patients are classified at admission and the level can be adjusted at any time by a physician generalist or oncologist based on the patient's clinical condition.

Level 1 — Standard Inpatient Care
  • Clinically stable patients requiring hospitalization and routine oncology care
  • Routine monitoring
  • Standard nursing and physician support
Level 2 — Enhanced Monitoring & Stabilization
  • Patients who are less stable or require stabilization before chemotherapy, radiotherapy or surgery
  • Closer observation
  • More frequent nursing and physician assessment
Level 3 — High-Acuity Care
  • Sicker patients requiring frequent clinical assessment and substantially closer monitoring
  • High level of nursing support
  • Frequent physician review
Level 4 — Intensive Care
  • Critically ill patients requiring the highest level of monitoring and medical/nursing support
  • Continuous monitoring
  • Intensive physician and nursing care
