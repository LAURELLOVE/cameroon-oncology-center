Surgical Oncology

Specialized Cancer Surgery. Multidisciplinary Care.

At Cameroon Oncology Center, our surgical oncology team provides safe, high-quality, patient-centered surgical care for a wide range of cancers. We work closely with medical oncology, radiation oncology, imaging, pathology, anesthesia and nursing to deliver the best possible outcomes.
