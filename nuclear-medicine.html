Nuclear Medicine

Molecular Imaging. Better Diagnosis. More Informed Cancer Care.

At Cameroon Oncology Center, our nuclear medicine service uses small amounts of radioactive material (radiotracers) to show how the body is functioning at a cellular level. This helps to detect disease early, stage cancer, guide treatment, and monitor response with precision and care.

Our Nuclear Medicine Services

Oncology & Cancer Imaging
  • Cancer staging and restaging
  • Detection of metastatic disease
  • Bone scintigraphy for bone metastases, fracture and tumors
  • Sentinel lymph node imaging
  • Assessment of treatment response and recurrence
  • Evaluation of tumors (e.g. pancreas, adrenal glands)
Cardiac Nuclear Medicine
  • Myocardial perfusion imaging
  • Assessment of blood flow and heart function
  • Detection of coronary artery disease
  • Evaluation of heart function after heart attack
  • MUGA scans before and after chemotherapy
Renal Nuclear Medicine
  • Evaluation of kidney function and urinary tract obstruction
  • Assessment of hypertension in the kidney arteries
  • Evaluation for kidney infection
  • Assessment of abnormal urine flow (reflux)
Thyroid & Endocrine Imaging
  • Diagnosis of thyroid nodules and thyroid cancer
  • Assessment of hyperthyroidism
  • Parathyroid adenoma localization
  • Evaluation of endocrine disorders
Other Applications
  • Brain imaging for seizures, memory loss and blood flow problems
  • Gastrointestinal studies (e.g. gastric emptying)
  • Lung ventilation/perfusion (V/Q)
  • Infection and inflammation imaging
  • Hematologic imaging

Patient Information & Safety

  • Uses small amounts of radioactive material (radiotracers)
  • Examinations are generally safe with a low radiation risk
  • Please inform us if you are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Tell your doctor about all medications, allergies and recent illnesses
  • Most procedures are outpatient, non-invasive and usually painless
