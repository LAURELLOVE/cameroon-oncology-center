Meet Our Specialist
FE
Dr. Fidele EyebeHead, Psycho-Oncology & Psychology Services

Dr. Fidele Eyebe leads our Psycho-Oncology and Psychology services, providing expert care to patients with cancer and their families. He is committed to integrating mental health into comprehensive cancer care to improve emotional well-being, treatment adherence and quality of life.

How We Can Help
  • Anxiety and stress at diagnosis
  • Depression and low mood
  • Coping with treatment and side effects
  • Fear of recurrence
  • Body image and self-esteem concerns
  • Family and caregiver counseling
  • Financial and social stress
  • End-of-life and bereavement support
  • Building resilience and hope
Our Services
  • Psychological assessment and distress screening
  • Individual and group counseling
  • Psychoeducation for patients and families
  • Emotional and motivational support
  • Crisis intervention
  • Coping-skills development
  • Family counseling and social support
  • Support during active treatment
  • Survivorship support
  • Palliative and end-of-life psychological care
  • Referral to other services when needed
