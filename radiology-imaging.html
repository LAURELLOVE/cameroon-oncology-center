Our Services

Radiology & Diagnostic Imaging

Clear Images. Confident Decisions. Brighter Tomorrows.

At Cameroon Oncology Center, our radiology and imaging services provide high-quality, reliable imaging to support cancer diagnosis, treatment planning, response assessment and long-term follow-up. We work closely with our multidisciplinary team to deliver accurate, timely and patient-centered care.

Our Imaging Services

Computed Tomography (CT)

Computed Tomography (CT)

  • Diagnostic CT scans for cancer diagnosis and staging
  • High-resolution, multi-slice imaging
  • CT-guided procedures (biopsy, drainage)
  • Assessment of treatment response
  • Whole body and specialized studies
Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)

Hitachi AIRIS Elite 0.35 T Open MRI

  • Open-design MRI for improved comfort
  • Brain, spine and selected body imaging
  • Cancer diagnosis, staging and follow-up
  • Soft tissue evaluation
  • Preoperative and treatment-planning support
Ultrasound (US)

Ultrasound (US)

  • Abdominal and pelvic ultrasound
  • Breast and thyroid ultrasound
  • Image-guided procedures
  • Vascular and Doppler studies
  • Follow-up and surveillance imaging
CT Simulation for Radiotherapy

CT Simulation for Radiotherapy

  • Philips Big Bore CT Simulator
  • High-precision imaging for radiotherapy planning
  • Immobilization and positioning systems
  • Accurate target delineation
  • Supports advanced 3D, IMRT and other radiation techniques
Clinical Applications
  • Cancer diagnosis and staging
  • Assessment of metastatic disease
  • Monitoring treatment response
  • Pre-operative and pre-radiotherapy planning
  • Detection of recurrence
  • Evaluation of treatment-related complications
  • Image-guided interventions
  • Long-term surveillance
