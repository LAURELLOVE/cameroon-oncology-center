"Together We Bring Hope Closer to Home."

One Network. One Goal. A Cancer-Free Cameroon.

Our Vision

To be the leading cancer care network in Cameroon, providing high-quality, accessible and compassionate care to every patient, everywhere.

Our Mission

To improve access to comprehensive cancer care through collaborative partnerships with physicians who share responsibility for early diagnosis, referral, treatment coordination, survivorship and palliative care.

Guiding Principles

  • Patient welfare first
  • Respect patient choice
  • Clinical independence
  • Confidentiality
  • Evidence-based care
  • Ethical collaboration

The COC Affiliate Care Pathway

A Continuous, Shared-Care Journey

1
Affiliate Physician (Initial Evaluation)
  • Recognizes suspected cancer
  • Initiates work-up
  • Counsels the patient
  • Prepares a complete referral package
2
Referral (to COC)
  • Clinical history
  • Pathology reports
  • Imaging and laboratory results
  • Specific referral question
3
Cameroon Oncology Center (Specialized Care)
  • Patient navigation
  • Multidisciplinary review
  • Advanced diagnostics
  • Radiotherapy, chemotherapy, surgery
  • Supportive care
4
Shared Plan (Joint Management)
  • Treatment plan
  • Communication with affiliate physician
  • Defined follow-up strategy
  • Patient education
5
Local Follow-up (Closer to Home)
  • Surveillance and follow-up
  • Supportive care
  • Symptom control
  • Rapid re-escalation to COC when specialist review is needed

Continuous Communication, Treatment Updates and Shared Decision Support

The patient remains at the Center or Spoke Center

Patient choice · Clinical independence · Confidentiality · Documented shared care · No care merely for referral

Four Affiliate Levels

Level 1 — Referral Partner
  • General practitioners and family physicians
  • Early recognition
  • Initial work-up
  • Early referral
  • Basic follow-up
  • Community education
Level 2 — Service Partner
  • District and regional hospitals
  • Coordinate diagnostics
  • Prepare referral packages
  • Follow patients after treatment
  • Manage supportive care
Level 3 — Collaborative Care Partner
  • Specialists (surgery, gynecology, urology, ENT, gastroenterology, etc.)
  • Co-management
  • Joint tumor board participation
  • Shared care and follow-up
  • Training and education
Level 4 — Advanced Collaborative Partner
  • Comprehensive oncology services
  • Strong integration with COC
  • Joint programs and research
  • Training and capacity building
  • Leadership in regional cancer care
