Our Services

Urology & Urologic Oncology

Comprehensive Care for the Urinary Tract and Male Reproductive Health

At Cameroon Oncology Center, we provide expert, compassionate and patient-centered urologic care for both cancerous and non-cancerous conditions of the kidneys, ureters, bladder, prostate, testicles and male reproductive system. Our multidisciplinary team works together to deliver accurate diagnosis, advanced treatment options and better outcomes.
