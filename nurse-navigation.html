Oncology Nurse Navigation

No Patient Navigates Cancer Care Alone.

At Cameroon Oncology Center, every oncology patient is supported by a dedicated oncology nurse navigator from the first point of contact through all stages of care. Our Universal Closed-Loop Oncology Nurse Navigation Program ensures that each patient receives personalized guidance, education, coordination and ongoing support.

The COC Closed-Loop Oncology Nurse Navigation Pathway

A Continuous, Patient-Centered Navigation Cycle

1 · Registration
  • Patient arrives at COC (from any source)
  • Registration and medical record creation
  • Initial orientation to services
2 · Entry Navigation (Before Consultation)
  • Warm welcome and comprehensive assessment
  • Review of medical records and previous investigations
  • Identify clinical and non-clinical barriers (financial, transportation, psychosocial, etc.)
  • Organize laboratory, imaging and other tests
  • Prepare patient for specialist consultation
3 · Specialist Consultation
  • Consultation with the appropriate specialist(s) (Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Surgery, Gynecology, etc.)
  • Diagnosis and staging
  • Discussion of treatment options
  • Development of individualized care plan
4 · Exit Navigation (After Consultation)
  • Review and explain treatment plan
  • Teach-back to confirm understanding
  • Arrange appointments and referrals
  • Coordinate additional tests
  • Address patient questions and concerns
  • Provide emotional support
5 · Treatment Coordination
  • Coordinate start of treatment (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, surgery, etc.)
  • Liaise with pharmacy, laboratory, imaging and other services
  • Monitor treatment schedule
  • Manage side effects and complications
  • Ensure access to supportive care (nutrition, psychology, cardiology, palliative care, etc.)
6 · Longitudinal Follow-up (Continuous Navigation)
  • Regular contact (calls, visits, WhatsApp)
  • Monitor symptoms and side effects
  • Ensure adherence to treatment
  • Assist with survivorship care planning
  • Support during recurrence or palliation
  • Link to community resources as needed

The navigator remains connected to the patient throughout the entire cancer journey.

Before You Leave COC

See Your Nurse Navigator Again

Every oncology patient returns to the nurse navigator after the specialist consultation. The navigator reviews the recommendations, confirms understanding, arranges appointments and referrals, and addresses any remaining barriers before you leave.

What Your Oncology Nurse Navigator Does

  • Comprehensive patient assessment
  • Review and organize medical records
  • Coordinate investigations and appointments
  • Provide education and emotional support
  • Identify and help overcome barriers to care (financial, transportation, language, etc.)
  • Work closely with the multidisciplinary team
  • Ensure continuous follow-up
