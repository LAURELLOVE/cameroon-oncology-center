Your First Visit to COC

Please bring the following, when available:

  • Referral letter or physician notes
  • Pathology/biopsy reports
  • Previous laboratory results
  • CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray or other imaging and reports
  • Previous treatment records (surgery, chemotherapy or radiotherapy)
  • Current medication list
  • Identification and insurance/authorization documents
Do not delay seeking care because your records are incomplete. Our team can evaluate you and help determine what additional information or testing is needed.

Financial Guidance & Access to Care

Talk to Us About the Cost of Your Care

Our team will help you understand treatment costs, insurance/authorization requirements and available financial-support pathways. Please discuss any financial concerns with us before delaying or abandoning treatment.

Our goal is to help you get the care you need.

Cancer Care Is a Family Journey

We welcome family members and caregivers as partners in care. Families can receive treatment education, medication guidance, nutrition advice, psychological support and instructions for caring for the patient between visits.

When Hospitalization Is Needed

  • 90% private-room hospitalization
  • Maximum 1 nurse : 6 patients
  • 24-hour onsite physician generalist
  • Twice-daily oncologist review
  • Four levels of acuity-based care
