Medical Oncology

Comprehensive Cancer Care. Personalized for You.

At Cameroon Oncology Center, our medical oncology team provides evidence-based treatment for a wide range of cancers, using the latest therapies and a multidisciplinary approach to achieve the best possible outcomes. We combine clinical expertise, compassion and support to care for every patient throughout their cancer journey.

Our Medical Oncology Services

Chemotherapy

Standard and combination chemotherapy regimens for curative or palliative intent.

Targeted Therapy

Modern targeted therapies for selected cancers.

Immunotherapy

Immunotherapy for appropriate indications.

Hormonal Therapy

Hormone therapy for hormone-sensitive cancers (e.g. breast, prostate).

Supportive Care

Management of symptoms and treatment-related side effects.

Treatment Monitoring

Regular assessment of response and toxicity.

Patient Education

Information and counseling to help you understand your treatment.

Palliative & End-of-Life Care

Relief of symptoms and improved quality of life.
